Future Champions

How it works

  1. Nominate your brand’s up and coming marketer that would benefit from the networking and learning experience at the Australian Open.
  2. Nominees should be under 30 or under years old at the time of the Australian Open 2027.
  3. The trip would take place over 19th, 20th, 21st January 2027. The experience includes:
    • 3 nights' accommodation in central Melbourne
    • Return economy flights from the nearest airport that Emirates flies from
    • Ground transfers
    • Tennis tickets, hospitality, AO Matters marketing conference attendance and merchandise
  4. Submissions close August 15th 2026. A panel consisting of Tennis Australia Leaders (Chief Commercial Officer, Director Partnerships, Head of Marketing) will vote for most worthy recipients from a shortlisted 30 to select 6 invitees. A blend of markets and sectors will be represented in final pool.
  5. Winners will be notified by September 1 and travel arranged in consultation with invitees. Any guests or elongation of travel will be at invitees' own expense.
  6. The invitee would consent to content being created around the experience for use in Tennis Australia’s social channels (eg Linkedin).

Any questions should be directed to futurechampions@tennis.com.au.
